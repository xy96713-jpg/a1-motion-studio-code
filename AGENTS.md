## Frames: fixed division of labour

Every frame is built in layers (SYSTEM_MAP.md, 画面分工):
- Art (plates, paper, lighting, perspective, props): GPT image generation, text-free; props as separate transparent layers. Themes declare which card parts are art (`artLibrary` / `artPlan`); the writer fills them.
- Facts (real people, screenshots, paper figures, footage, product images): real sources through the materials step. Never generated. Each piece of evidence in the storyboard names its form (photo, video, webpage, paper, logo, chart, recording), which decides where the materials step looks: web pages and papers through article source discovery (URL candidates; captured once checked: since 2026-10-23 the user does not confirm pages one by one — a page from a reputable outlet whose title names the case and whose text carries the fact is captured and used; user decision: 报道什么的不要总问我 确认合适的就找好做包装), photos and logos from image archives and the library, video from YouTube and archives, recordings from the author.
- Readable text, numbers and data graphics: code.
- Do not assemble art from basic shapes in code, and do not let the image model write readable text. A full-frame generation with text may be used only as a design sketch.
- Every number shown on a card must trace to the source text or to an archived source of the project (`facts` step; the shot's own source first): a web page checked as above (reputable outlet, names the case, its text carries the number; the user confirms on `/recipe-board/materials` only when they want to), captured by `page-capture.mjs` with screenshot, page text, URL and capture time (receipt in the NAS library, text in `materials/sources/`). The shot lists the sources it used (`factSources`). (User decision 2026-10-16.)

## Marks on page words

- Every bar, highlight, marker or box on the words of a real page (article, screen, the curled page, any new module) is drawn by [work/recipe-board-v1/modules/marks.js](work/recipe-board-v1/modules/marks.js) on boxes from `modules.mjs` `locatePhrases` (OCR, then fitted to the page's ink: `tightBox`). No module draws its own.
- The line measures every such mark after rendering (`markErrors`): it covers its glyphs and no neighbouring glyph or punctuation, has the line's height, its edges cross no other words, its sweep is finished and it is in the frame when complete. A failing mark fails the shot; fix the module, never loosen the check.
- A captured article is reflowed before it is shown (`articlePage`): only the article's lines, closed up where a picture or furniture was (the camera crossed a blank strip and jumped), no date line, no byline (user decision 2026-10-21: no 记者 on screen; a byline opening a paragraph is cut from its line), no disclaimer. The depth-of-field blur is a few screen pixels whatever the zoom; the big headline fades as the camera leaves it.
- The director's phrases are checked on their page before rendering (`phraseErrors`): on the page as printed (a phrase may wrap; the bar follows it), starting and ending on words.

## Quality complaints become checks

- Every shot module is measured by code before it renders (`framecheck.mjs`, `markErrors`; run at the still sheet, problems filed as the code's verdict with an owner: the module's faults go to `module-issues.json`, the director's back to the director; a shot with any does not render): type size (CJK 34 px a line, Latin 20), contrast, text cut by a frame edge at rest or in the subtitle band (below 935 px), texts over each other or over a face, text mostly hidden under a picture, quote marks with no words, Simplified characters or emoji, decorative lettering (`data-deco`) kept faint, a frame carrying less than half of what the reference's does at the same moment, a frame gone blank mid-shot, and every mark on page words. The geometry is measured every quarter second, the pixels at the key frames. Motion is measured on the animatic against the reference (a module that stands still where the reference moves holds the final render). The director's plan is checked for page phrases and torn labels (`labelErrors`: at most a third of the shots, never two in a row, two on a shot; house-rules.json `labels`).

- The reference is taken apart finely before a passage is planned (user decision 2026-10-21: 拆的不够细，很多没对标上动效、设计): `breakdown.mjs` measures every cut and, per sub-shot, the camera (zoom, pan, moving share, ease), the look (base, L*, chroma, grain), the display type (OCR height and place) and what appears; an agent reads each sub-shot (task `shotcard-<seg>`, rules `prompts/shotcard.md`: device, material, layers, type, motion, transition, texture), checked against the measures. The card is the reference's beats for the plan (the old inventory only cross-checks); the plan answers every item (`follows`: match, or differ with the reason); a cut said to match is a cut, a cut the reference does not make is said why; the reference's own cuts allow a 0.3 s flash and a second framing of the same subject. ## White model first (user decision 2026-10-22: 先低分辨率白膜做快速预览，确认了再渲染高质量)

- The order of a professional studio: what is cheap is decided where it is cheap. - A chapter is one take: a camera cut (a key of 0 s) inside a chapter stands only where the reference cuts at that moment, in the plan (`planCutErrors`) and in the module (`cutErrors`); a cut between shots rests, carries one move, whips, keeps the picture (match) or is covered (glitch) (`seamJerks`). ## Verification

- After any change to the card line, run `npm run test:recipe-board` (offline, about half a minute, sandboxed; it never writes project folders). Add a test there for each bug a run turns up.
- A new or changed chapter check is tried first on every chapter's kept geometry track (`node scripts/recipe-board.mjs replay --project <id> [--grep "<its message>"]`, seconds, no render): what it catches and what it wrongly catches are read there before the chapters run again (2026-10-26: two checks went out uncalibrated and cost a full re-render each).
- After code changes, run the narrowest meaningful check: `run --project <id> --dry`, a render of the affected shots, and a still regression (re-render the projects and compare; unchanged shots must stay pixel-identical).
- For documentation-only changes use `git diff --check` and link/path checks; trigger no generation.
- Report what was and was not verified.
